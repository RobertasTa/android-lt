Android LT — Lithuanian voice and dictation for your phone

A Lithuanian voice for Android phones: Ingutė, offline.

Your phone reads Lithuanian aloud, with clear s sounds and the stress set for 189,000 word forms.

Also ears: speak Lithuanian, the phone types — in SMS, notes, the ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini app.

Since 1.2.0 — two ready-made apps: download, install, done. No F-Droid, no cable, no computer.

What it is for

A phone that reads Lithuanian

Ingutė becomes the phone's text-to-speech voice, used by apps that read aloud: article and book readers, the screen reader.

Offline

The voice runs on the phone. The internet is needed once, to download the app and the voice.

Free

The voice and both apps are free and open source, with no ads and no accounts.

What it fixes

The missing s

The phone's pronunciation engine, eSpeak NG, often writes Lithuanian /s/ with a symbol Ingutė hardly learned. Without the fix "pasiimk skėtį" comes out as "paimk kėtį". The fix is in tokens.txt.

Stress

On its own, eSpeak NG puts the stress where the dictionary does in only 53 % of multi-syllable words: dãbar, lãbai, Lietùvos. With our lt_dict it is 99.3 %: dabar̃, labaĩ, Lietuvõs.

A Lithuanian speech recognition model listened to 12 everyday sentences. Word errors: 7.7 % without fixes, 4.4 % with the s fix, 1.1 % with the stress list as well. Tested on a Xiaomi 12T Pro, Android 15, SherpaTTS 3.4.

Installing (about 5 minutes)

Android 10 or newer (Android 12 for the ears). Both files are in the release.

Voice: "Ingutė" (Ingute-1.0.apk, 151 MB)

  1. Download Ingute-1.0.apk to the phone and tap it (in the browser or a file manager, in Download). The phone asks whether to allow installing from this source: allow it.
  2. Install. No need to open the app: Ingutė installs herself, with the s fix and the stress dictionary.
  3. Phone settings → "Text-to-speech output" → engine Ingutė, language Lithuanian → "Listen to an example".

Ears: "Parakeetype LT" (Parakeetype-LT-0.5.0-lt2.apk, 59 MB)

  1. Download and install. If Google Play Protect offers to scan the app, tap Scan, then Install (for us it answered that the app looks safe).
  2. Open it, allow the microphone, and next to "Model not installed" tap Install.
  3. On the "Parakeet Lithuanian (Default)" card: Download in browser (955 MB, Wi-Fi is better) → when it is done, Import model file → parakeet-lt-kmynas-int8.zip. A minute later: Active model; you can delete the archive.
  4. On the start screen, next to "Keyboard not enabled" → Open settings → turn Parakeetype on. Android warns that the keyboard "may collect everything you type": it says this for every keyboard, and this one has no internet permission, so the text never leaves the phone → OK.
  5. Dictate: in any text field long-press 🌐 in Gboard → Parakeetype Voice Input, hold the microphone button and speak.

Both apps are signed with our key (certificate SHA-256 8679aa5f…afaf7f); file hashes are in SHA256SUMS.txt in the release. The app screens are in Lithuanian, the system dialogs in Russian (the phone's language). Tested on a Xiaomi 12T Pro, Android 15.

Advanced: the voice through SherpaTTS from F-Droid (without our app, about 10 minutes)
  1. Install SherpaTTS from F-Droid. It is not on Google Play; a search there shows other, ad-supported apps. File: org.woheller69.ttsengine_34.apk (3.4, 91 MB). The right app is called SherpaTTS and has no ads.
  2. Download to the phone from the release: lt_LT-ingute-medium.onnx and tokens.txt.
  3. SherpaTTS → "INSTALL PIPER MODEL FROM SD": language code lit, name Ingute, then the model and tokens files → OK.
  4. Phone settings → "Text-to-speech output" → engine SherpaTTS, language Lithuanian → "Listen to an example".

Ingutė now speaks with the s fix. The stress list takes one more step, from a computer.

5. Stress, from a computer

Recent Android versions do not let file managers write into another app's folder, so lt_dict goes in with Google's adb. On the phone: Settings → About phone → tap the build number 7 times → in developer options turn on USB debugging → connect the cable and allow it.

First check that your SherpaTTS dictionary is the original (it must be 5426503daf0a29836d0f168d38b38581; if not, do not replace it):

adb shell md5sum /sdcard/Android/data/org.woheller69.ttsengine/files/espeak-ng-data/lt_dict

Then a backup, the new dictionary and a restart of the app:

adb shell cp /sdcard/Android/data/org.woheller69.ttsengine/files/espeak-ng-data/lt_dict /sdcard/Android/data/org.woheller69.ttsengine/files/espeak-ng-data/lt_dict_orig
adb push lt_dict /sdcard/Android/data/org.woheller69.ttsengine/files/espeak-ng-data/lt_dict
adb shell am force-stop org.woheller69.ttsengine

Listen to "Dabar labai gera diena, nes Lietuvos rinktinė laimėjo." To undo, rename lt_dict_orig back to lt_dict. Reinstalling SherpaTTS undoes this step.

Ears: Lithuanian dictation

Speak Lithuanian to the phone and the text appears in any text field. The open-source voice keyboard Parakeetype (theScrabi) does this: it recognises speech on the phone and has no internet permission. Its default model hears Lithuanian poorly; our archive parakeet-lt-kmynas-int8.zip (955 MB) gives it Lithuanian ears. Our "Parakeetype LT" is the same app with the Lithuanian model first and as the default.

Everyday use

Installing does not yet make the phone speak or listen. Here is what to set up once and how to use it afterwards. Menu names differ a little between phones, so the easiest way to find them is the search box in the phone's settings. The screenshots are from a Xiaomi phone set to Russian.

Make the phone read with Ingutė's voice

  1. Ingutė as the phone's voice (once). Search the settings for "text-to-speech". Preferred engine → Ingutė (or SherpaTTS if you took the advanced route; Android warns the engine "may collect text"; it shows this for every non-Google voice; tap OK). Language → Lithuanian. Play: Ingutė should speak.
  2. Have any text on the screen read aloud (once). Search the settings for "Select to Speak", turn it on and allow it to see the screen. A blue button appears at the edge of the screen.
  3. Every day: open an SMS, an email or an article → tap the blue button → tap a paragraph and Ingutė reads it (▶ for the whole screen, ■ to stop).

Make the phone type what you say (ears)

  1. Open the place where you type: SMS, notes, search.
  2. Switch the keyboard to Parakeetype: in Gboard, long-press 🌐 → "Parakeetype Voice Input".
  3. Hold the microphone button and speak. When you let go, the text appears; the app adds full stops, commas and capital letters itself.
  4. Back to your usual keyboard: the small keyboard icon on the right, above Enter.

Privacy, plainly

Text and audio stay on the phone: the voice and the ears run there. "Ingutė" (like SherpaTTS) goes online only when you ask it to download another language from its list; Ingutė herself is already inside. "Parakeetype LT" has no internet permission at all: the browser downloads the model archive.

Limits

  • Numbers and abbreviations are read by eSpeak NG itself ("15:25 val." may sound odd).
  • Only the stressed syllable is set, not the Lithuanian pitch accent.
  • About 9 % of words are not in the dictionary; eSpeak NG guesses those.
  • lt_dict was built for SherpaTTS 3.4; check the md5 first on other versions.
  • The SherpaTTS developers do not agree with Google's developer identity requirement, planned worldwide for 2027; the app warns about it itself. Both our apps show the same warning of theirs; what we do about it we will decide before 2027.
  • It is a synthetic voice: natural, but not a person.
